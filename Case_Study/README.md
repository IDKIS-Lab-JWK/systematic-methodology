# Case study: the 20 FlexiCT disagreements

The [21-page supplementary case atlas](Updated_Supplementary_Case_Atlas.pdf) is
the PDF edition of the manuscript atlas, with all 20 cases and their review
summaries, three-plane attribution panels and CT overviews.

Start with [the complete 61-page report](FlexiCT_20_Case_Study.pdf). Each of the
20 public cases (R01-R20) includes its radiologist interpretation, three-plane
CT/Grad-CAM panel and 12-slice CT overview. The source figures are also supplied
individually under the latest publication notebook's supplementary figures.

The [four-page output guide](FlexiCT_Output_Guide.pdf) explains model-space
images, the separate heatmap scales, occlusion drops, fold consistency and the
limited randomized-classifier check. Original case identifiers were replaced
with public review labels in this guide.

These are all 20 disagreements from the FlexiCT final-block, five-fold ensemble
at the complete development size, among 278 held-out patients. The reread
includes label discrepancies, coexisting conditions, other disease and confirmed
model errors. It is descriptive post-test analysis; original labels and all
cases remain in performance calculations. R20 has provisional linkage.

The positive probability-drop criterion was met in 14/20 cases. A relative
top-mask-versus-random comparison was positive in 20/20, but six top-mask drops
were non-positive. Neither result establishes validated lesion localization.

Radiologist summaries are included as requested. Internal linkage/audit files
and verbatim correspondence are excluded. The report compiles existing public
figures and Tables S12-S14; it does not introduce a new diagnosis or experiment.

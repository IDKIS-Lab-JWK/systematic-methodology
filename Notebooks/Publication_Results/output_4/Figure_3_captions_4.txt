Figure 3a. Partial adaptation and frozen-encoder performance across development-set sizes. Macro-F1 is shown for 3DINO, COLIPRI, FlexiCT, Merlin, and Pillar-0 using five-fold CV ensembles at actual development-set sizes of 248, 456, 664, 872, and 1080 patients. Partial adaptation comprises 3DINO LoRA, final-block fine-tuning for COLIPRI, FlexiCT, and Pillar-0, and corrected Merlin ConvLoRA with layer-wise learning-rate decay (LLRD). Frozen-encoder comparators use linear probing, except COLIPRI, which uses its native pooling and classifier procedure. The completed 3DINO linear-probe results are included. Points are observed ensemble estimates, and lines connect them for readability; no monotonic model or extrapolation was fitted. The same 278-patient test cohort was used throughout. Perforation support in development remained 40 patients at every size, so both total sample size and class composition changed. Curves show point estimates without confidence bands. Full-network Merlin fine-tuning is reported separately in Figure 3b.

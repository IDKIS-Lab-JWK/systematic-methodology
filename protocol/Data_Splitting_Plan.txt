Five-fold cross-validation JSON representation:
- The 70/30 Train/Val rows above are retained as historical planning values;
  the JSON manifests replace that single split with five CV folds.
- The DICOM, NIfTI, and PNG folders remain physically unsplit.
- Patient assignments are stored in Dataset/Splits/*.json.
- Each percentage's development pool is partitioned into five deterministic,
  class-stratified, mutually exclusive folds.
- For iteration i, fold i is validation and the other four folds are training.
- Each hyperparameter candidate is evaluated across these five iterations.
- The fixed held-out test set is identical for all percentages and is used
  only once after model and hyperparameter selection.
- Seed: 42.
- Lower-percentage patient pools are subsets of higher-percentage pools.
